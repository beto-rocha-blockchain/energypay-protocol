# Competitor Landscape Notes

This file is a working map for validation, not a claim that the listed categories are direct one-to-one competitors.

## Competitive Categories

### Legacy Energy Settlement And Market Operators

These systems coordinate energy market settlement, reporting and reconciliation through established institutional workflows. They are credible, trusted and compliant, but usually move slower than programmable financial infrastructure.

EnergyPay differentiation:

- Faster programmable settlement.
- Ledger-level audit trail.
- API-native reconciliation.
- Stablecoin-compatible treasury rails.

### Energy Trading And Retail Platforms

Energy retailers, comercializadoras and trading desks often use specialized platforms for contracts, positions, invoices and risk. These tools are close to users' daily workflows but generally rely on traditional banking and reconciliation rails.

EnergyPay differentiation:

- Settlement execution as a product layer, not just record keeping.
- Direct integration with Stellar payments.
- P2P and contract-level transaction receipts.

### Blockchain Payment Rails

General-purpose blockchain payment systems can move value quickly, but they are not tailored to energy contracts, PLD references, counterparty limits, submarket risk or energy reconciliation workflows.

EnergyPay differentiation:

- Energy-native operating model.
- Contract registry and settlement lifecycle.
- Institutional UI for risk, audit, treasury and operations.

### Energy Data API Providers

Data providers monetize market data, grid telemetry and analytics, but payment and entitlement flows are often separate from financial settlement.

EnergyPay differentiation:

- Future x402-style monetization for energy APIs.
- Unified payment, audit and entitlement trail.
- Path from data access to settlement automation.

## Validation Questions

- Which user segment feels the settlement pain most acutely: retailers, traders, generators or API consumers?
- Is the initial wedge P2P payments, contract settlement, treasury automation or paid APIs?
- What compliance and custody concerns block adoption?
- Which existing workflow must EnergyPay integrate with first?

## Positioning Hypothesis

EnergyPay should avoid positioning as a generic crypto payment app. The stronger wedge is "programmable settlement infrastructure for energy market operations", with Stellar as the execution rail and institutional reconciliation as the trust layer.
