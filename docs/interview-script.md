# Customer Interview Script — EnergyPay

## Objective
Understand how energy market participants handle settlement, payments and data.

## Questions

1. How do you currently settle energy contracts?
2. What are the main challenges in settlement?
3. How long does settlement usually take?
4. What risks do you see in current processes?
5. Do you use any automation or APIs?
6. How do you handle energy data?
7. Would faster settlement impact your operations?
8. Would you pay for programmable settlement or automation?
9. What tools do you currently use?
10. What would make your life easier in this process?

## Target Profiles
- Energy traders
- Comercializadoras
- Free market consumers
